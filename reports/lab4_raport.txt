Task 1:
ps -eo pid,comm | grep sleep
   7541 sleep
   7544 sleep
   8579 sleep
ps -eo pid,args | grep sleep
   7541 sleep 1000
   7542 bash -c while true; do sleep 0.5; done
   7544 sleep 1000
   8595 sleep 0.5
   8597 grep --color=auto sleep

we can notice that comm returns only the executed process, 
while args returns the process with its parameters
